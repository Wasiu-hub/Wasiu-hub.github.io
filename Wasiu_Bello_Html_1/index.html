Inception

Director: Christopher Nolan

Genre: Science Fiction / Thriller

Year Released: 2010

Awards: Academy Award Winner
Fun Fact: The spinning top scene is still debated!

Inception is a mind-bending film that explores dreams within dreams.
The story follows a skilled thief who enters people's subconscious to steal secrets. One key theme is the blurred line between reality and illusion. The dream world becomes an unpredictable landscape.
